What's KeepyCash?

The spend tracker that tracks your debt too.

KeepyCash is a simple iPhone and Android app for tracking what you spend and what you owe. No bank linking, no ads, and everything is included in one app.

Spend tracker

Log a spend in seconds.

Tap the big plus, punch in the amount and who you paid. KeepyCash remembers your payees and picks the category for you, so most purchases are logged before you've left the checkout.

  • Split one receipt across categories
  • Mark bills as recurring so they log themselves
  • Cash counts just like card
Insights

See where it all goes.

Plain-English charts show money in, money out and what's left, with your biggest categories and top payees at a glance. No accounting degree needed.

  • Money in vs. money out, in one picture
  • Overspend alerts before a category runs dry
Debt tracker

Every card and loan, one screen.

Credit cards, car loans, personal loans, store cards. Add them all and see what you still owe, what you've paid off, and the interest you've saved so far.

  • Progress bar and interest rate on every debt
  • A running "interest saved" number
  • A big green Paid off moment when you clear one
Payoff journey

Every payment is a step toward sunshine.

Each debt gets its own path, from the rain cloud where you started to the sun at the finish line. Every payment you log fills in more of the road, and you can see your debt-free date.

Small wins

"$6.50 gone. That's $0.79 in interest you'll never pay."

Every payment shows the interest it just wiped out, plus a share button for the wins your audience will want to cheer on.
