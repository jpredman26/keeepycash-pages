Straight answers

Is KeepyCash a spend tracker or a debt tracker?

Both, in one app. The App Store and Google Play list it as a spend tracker, and the Debt Tracker is built in. You don't need a second app or a second purchase.

Why pay upfront instead of a free trial?

Most "free trials" are built around you forgetting to cancel. We'd rather be straight with you: pay once, get everything, no countdown timer and no surprise charge after a trial.

How do I get the app after I buy?

Check your email after checkout, then download KeepyCash from the App Store or Google Play and sign in. Your full access is waiting.

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. KeepyCash never asks for your bank login. You log spending and payments yourself by typing, voice, receipt scan or statement screenshot. It takes seconds, and your data stays private.

Can I track cash spending too?

Yes. Because you log spending yourself instead of linking a bank, cash counts exactly like card. Type it or say it and it's in.

What kinds of debt can I track?

Any debt with a balance: credit cards, car loans, personal loans, store cards and more. Add the balance and interest rate, and KeepyCash tracks your progress and the interest each payment saves.

Is the debt tracker a payoff calculator?

It's a tracker. Instead of a one-off calculation you forget about, you add each balance and interest rate once, then log payments as you make them. KeepyCash keeps your balance, percent paid off and interest saved up to date.

Isn't logging everything by hand a pain?

It's quicker than you'd think. Most purchases take a few seconds, and you can snap a statement screenshot to log a whole batch at once.

Is my bank statement screenshot stored?

No. The image is read to pull out your transactions and then discarded. Nothing is kept.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Native apps for both, with cloud sync so your numbers stay current across your devices.

Does it renew?

Yes, once a year. Turn off renewal any time using the manage-subscription link in your confirmation email. A couple of clicks, no phone call, no maze.
